Everything can have or be a clinical pattern. It could be the pattern of someone sitting crossed-legged way too long every day. It could also be the pattern of a skin lesion in a specific part of the body. Which patterns are useful? Those that are common. Those that inform treatment well. Those that correspond well to a certain prognosis or even to a diagnosis. 

If we are talking structures, we could have a specific pattern for every nerve, muscles, tendon, ligament, bone. But this only makes sense if we can go one step back and identify clinical patterns for any of these structures in general, not one specifically.  And then a way of deducing the specific structure, e.g. through palpation and anatomical knowledge in addition to the general features.

Patterns that have no clear relationship with anything structural need to be common before all else. There is no other reason to give them a specific name. In all other cases, we just need to list all clinical signs that seem relevant.
